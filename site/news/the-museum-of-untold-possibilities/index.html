The Museum of Untold Possibilities

Epic MegaGrantUnreal EngineVRQuestEye Pilots

Here it is, the big one. I've submitted The Museum of Untold Possibilities to the Epic MegaGrants program and I've put the full pitch up on the site for anyone who wants to read it.

The short version: CoCo VR meets the World of Sketchbot. A non-game VR museum on Quest where you wander galleries of robots at your own pace, timed exhibitions bring you back, and an Art Lab lets you build your own custom 'bot, export it for 3D printing and view it in AR. Basically everything I've loved about custom toy shows and 3D printing, but virtual.

I'm not doing this alone. Erik Anderson is on board as Unreal engineer and Erik Desiderio is handling music and sound. Two Eriks, one museum.

Fingers crossed! Either way, the museum is getting built. Read the whole pitch on the project page.
