Sketchbot, the designer vinyl toy

The 5.5-inch retro robot with a removable pencil, produced by Sketchbot Studios since 2010. Colourway variants, resin exclusives and 3D-printable robots have shipped through My Plastic Heart, Crewest, DesignerCon and Mold3D.
