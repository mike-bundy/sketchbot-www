TRON Immersive

An homage to the 1982 original, built as the studio's first spatial website environment for Apple Vision Pro. Step onto The Grid from Safari, with a Recognizer and a Tank rendered stereoscopically in the page.

The original TRON ignited my passion for computer animation when I first saw it at 16 in 1982. With the release of TRON: Ares, I decided to create an homage to the original as a proof-of-concept for my first spatial website environment for Apple Vision Pro.

Modeled and animated in Autodesk Maya. Textured in Adobe Substance Painter. Assembled in Reality Composer Pro. END OF LINE.
