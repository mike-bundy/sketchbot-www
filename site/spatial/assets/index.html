Deliverables

What a spatial asset package contains

GeometryAll-quad subdivision cages with creases, plus baked SubD meshes at the level each target needs. Named, grouped, pivots at the floor, metres.
MaterialsUsdPreviewSurface for Quick Look and Safari; OpenPBR / RealityKit PBR variants where they matter. 2K textures by default, 4K on request, KTX2/ASTC on delivery.
AnimationSkeletal or transform animation in the USD stage, loop-clean, with idle and hero clips. Audio embedded where visionOS supports it.
PackagingUSDZ packages validated with usdchecker, size-budgeted per target (Safari inline, website environment, RealityKit app, Quick Look AR). Source USDA and Maya scenes included.
EnvironmentsFull-immersion scenes with baked GI, HDR environment maps, spatial audio loops and a lowered/scaled variant set for headset comfort.
Preview kitA drop-in web viewer (the code running on this page) with native <model>, three.js fallback and AR Quick Look links.

Authoring

Autodesk Maya, ZBrush, Adobe Substance 3D Painter, Reality Composer Pro, Quads on device.

Validation

Pixar USD tools, usdchecker, RealityKit and Quick Look passes on real Vision Pro hardware, Safari desktop and iOS.

Appeal

Thirty years of character work: silhouettes that read at a glance, materials that invite touch, scale that feels right in a room.
