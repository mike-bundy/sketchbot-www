About

Steve Talkowski

A veteran of the computer animation scene: Art Director, Animation Director, character designer and animator on hundreds of national brands, senior animator at Blue Sky Studios on Ice Age, Joe's Apartment, Alien Resurrection and the Academy Award-winning short Bunny. Founded Sketchbot Studios in 2008 and self-produced the Sketchbot designer toy.
