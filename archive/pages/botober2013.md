# Botober 2013 — Sketchbot Studios

**URL:** https://www.sketchbot.tv/botober2013

**Description:**  These were my contributions to the daily #Botober sketch challenge, which coincided with #Inktober. &nbsp;I would do a 20 minute sketch first thing in the morning, then jump into Maya to create the 3D version. &nbsp;Imported these models into Keyshot3D for final render. 

**Videos:**
- http://vimeo.com/stevetalkowski
