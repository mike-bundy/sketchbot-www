# TikiBot — Sketchbot Studios

**URL:** https://www.sketchbot.tv/tikibot

**Description:**  TikiBot is a tiki-inspired version of my Sketchbot designer toy. &nbsp;During my first visit to Maui last Fall, I was greatly inspired by the native tiki carvings we encountered. &nbsp;A few sketches and I landed on a version that I wanted to explore in 3d. &nbsp;I used my base mesh of the original Sketchbot toy and began modifying and adding to it in Maya, knowing that I was prepping the geometry for import to ZBrush, where I would undertake the bulk of the digital "carving". &nbsp;After polypainting the textures inside ZBrush, it was a simple matter of exporting into the incredible Keyshot3d rendering software and experimenting with the final finish and render.  The 12 subtools (comprised of:&nbsp;body, upper teeth, lower teeth, tongue, left arm, left hand, right arm, right hand, spear, grass skirt, left leg, right leg)&nbsp;were setup with a key system and hollowed out via ZBrush's Decimation Master and 3d Print Export plug-ins. &nbsp;I then uploaded to Shapeways and output two different sizes for use as prototypes and display models at the 2014 3
